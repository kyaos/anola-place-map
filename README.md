# Anola Place – Building Map (rough prototype)

A schematic 3D map of Anola Place (2041 Bellwood Avenue, Burnaby) for pointing out **where** something is during a meeting: click the building to drop a red pointer, type a unit or place in **Go to…**, or press **▶ Tour**.

> Schematic model – not to scale. A communication aid only; not an architectural, engineering, survey or legal drawing.

> **Unofficial prototype.** Made by a volunteer as a demonstration. Not produced or endorsed by Strata Plan NWS2020, its council or its property manager. The building model is simplified and may be wrong. The three issues shown are made-up examples and do not describe real problems at Anola Place. Do not rely on this for buying, selling, insurance, legal or engineering decisions.

- Runs entirely in the browser. Issues you record are saved only in your own browser; nothing is uploaded.
- The three issues it starts with are **made-up examples**.
